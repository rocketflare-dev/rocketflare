# Master Services Agreement

Brightside Grounds & Gardens Ltd (company number 08812345), Unit 4, Ferris Lane Industrial Estate, Ashcombe ("Brightside", "we").

2025 edition. These terms apply to every site maintenance contract we sign from 1 July 2025. Each customer signs a short Site Schedule that names the site, the plan, the fee and anything that differs from these terms. Where the Site Schedule and these terms disagree, the Site Schedule wins.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Customer** means the business or organisation named in the Site Schedule.

1.2 **Site** means the land and buildings described in the Site Schedule, including car parks, footpaths and access roads within the boundary shown on the site plan.

1.3 **Plan** means one of the maintenance plans described in our current Service Catalogue: Essential, Standard or Premium.

1.4 **Monthly Fee** means the fixed monthly amount for the Plan stated in the Site Schedule.

1.5 **Additional Works** means any work not included in the Plan, charged at the Service Catalogue price or as separately quoted.

1.6 **Visit Day** means the weekday on which routine visits normally take place, stated in the Site Schedule.

1.7 **Winter Season** means 1 November to 31 March.

## 2. Term and renewal

2.1 The contract starts on the start date in the Site Schedule and runs for an initial term of twelve months.

2.2 At the end of the initial term the contract renews automatically for further periods of twelve months unless either party gives at least 60 days' written notice before the renewal date.

2.3 The renewal date is stated in the Site Schedule. If it is not stated, it is the day before the anniversary of the start date.

2.4 We may change the Monthly Fee at renewal by giving at least 30 days' written notice. If the Customer does not accept the change, it may end the contract at the renewal date without penalty.

## 3. Our services

3.1 We will provide the services included in the Plan at the frequency set out in the Service Catalogue, normally on the Visit Day.

3.2 We will provide Additional Works only when the Customer has asked for them in writing (email is enough) or accepted a quote.

3.3 We may change the Visit Day on 14 days' notice where our rounds change. We will try to keep the same day for each site and will agree any permanent change with the Customer.

3.4 Weather: we will not carry out work that would damage the Site or put our staff at risk. A visit lost to weather will be rescheduled where practical. Missed visits are credited only as set out in the Service Catalogue.

3.5 Winter gritting: where the Plan or Site Schedule includes winter gritting, we will grit the areas shown on the site plan whenever the forecast road-surface temperature is at or below 1°C, without needing a call from the Customer. We do not guarantee that surfaces will be free of ice and the Customer remains responsible for warning signs and closing unsafe areas.

## 4. The Customer's responsibilities

4.1 Give our staff safe access to the Site on the Visit Day, including gate codes and keys where needed.

4.2 Tell us about hazards: buried services, ponds, asbestos, aggressive animals, events that will close car parks.

4.3 Move vehicles from areas to be gritted or cleared where reasonably possible.

4.4 Name a contact for day-to-day matters. The Customer may change its contact by telling us in writing.

## 5. Fees and payment

5.1 The Monthly Fee is invoiced on the first working day of each month in advance.

5.2 Additional Works are invoiced monthly in arrears.

5.3 Unless the Site Schedule states otherwise, invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date.

5.4 We may charge interest on late payments at 4% above the Bank of England base rate under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, and may suspend services if an invoice is more than 30 days overdue after a written reminder.

5.5 All prices are exclusive of VAT, which is charged at the prevailing rate.

5.6 Disputed invoices: the Customer must tell us within 14 days of the invoice date which items are disputed and why, and must pay the undisputed part on time.

## 6. Price changes

6.1 Catalogue prices for Additional Works may change once a year. Winter prices are reviewed each September.

6.2 The Monthly Fee changes only at renewal, as in clause 2.4, or when the Customer changes Plan.

## 7. Changing plans

7.1 The Customer may move to a higher Plan at any time from the first of the following month.

7.2 The Customer may move to a lower Plan only at the renewal date.

## 8. Insurance and liability

8.1 We hold public liability insurance of £5 million and employer's liability of £10 million. Certificates are available on request.

8.2 We are not liable for damage caused by the Customer's failure to tell us about a hazard under clause 4.2.

8.3 Our total liability in any contract year is limited to the fees paid in that year, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or anything else that cannot be limited by law.

8.4 We are not liable for loss of profits, loss of business or indirect loss.

## 9. Staff and subcontractors

9.1 Our staff are trained and hold the certificates needed for the work they do.

9.2 We may use subcontractors for specialist tree work and plant hire. We remain responsible for their work.

9.3 The Customer will not employ or engage any of our staff who worked on its Site during the contract, or for six months afterwards, without our agreement.

## 10. Data protection

10.1 We hold names, work emails and phone numbers of the Customer's contacts to run the contract. We do not hold personal data about the Customer's own customers, patients, pupils or residents.

10.2 Photographs taken on Site are for job records only and are never published without permission.

## 11. Ending the contract

11.1 Either party may end the contract by notice at the renewal date as in clause 2.2.

11.2 Either party may end the contract immediately by written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not put the breach right within 30 days of being asked to.

11.3 We may end the contract on 30 days' notice if invoices remain unpaid 60 days after their due date.

11.4 On ending, the Customer pays for services provided up to the end date and we return keys and delete gate codes.

## 12. Complaints

12.1 Complaints go first to the account manager named in the Site Schedule. If not resolved within 10 working days, to the Managing Director.

## 13. General

13.1 This agreement and the Site Schedule are the whole agreement between us.

13.2 Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties, except a change of contact under clause 4.4 or a change of Visit Day under clause 3.3, which may be made by email.

13.3 Notices may be sent by email to the addresses in the Site Schedule.

13.4 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
