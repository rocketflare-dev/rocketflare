# Brightside Grounds & Gardens — Staff Handbook

Version 3, June 2025. Owner: Dana Whitlock (Managing Director). Questions about anything in here go to Priya Nandakumar, Operations Manager, or to Lena Kowalczyk in the office.

This handbook replaces version 2 (March 2023). Please read it all, sign the last page and hand it to Lena. If something in here disagrees with your contract of employment, your contract wins.

## 1. About us

Brightside Grounds & Gardens Ltd looks after the outside of other people's businesses: lawns, beds, hedges, trees, car parks in winter. We have twelve staff, four vans, one tipper and a yard at Unit 4, Ferris Lane Industrial Estate, Ashcombe.

We work only for businesses and organisations. We do not take on private gardens, even for friends of staff — please do not quote for them on site.

## 2. Working hours

### 2.1 Normal hours

The working day is 07:30 to 16:00, Monday to Friday, with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Crews meet at the yard at 07:30 to load. If you are going straight to a site, tell Priya the day before.

### 2.2 Winter hours and gritting standby

From 1 November to 31 March there is a gritting standby rota. When you are on standby you must be reachable by phone from 03:00 and able to reach the yard within 40 minutes. Standby is paid at a flat £35 per night whether or not you are called out, plus overtime for hours worked. Swaps are fine but must go through Priya, not the WhatsApp group.

### 2.3 Overtime

Overtime is paid at time and a half for weekday hours beyond 16:00 and double time on Sundays and bank holidays. Overtime must be agreed by Priya or Dana before you work it, except during a gritting call-out.

## 3. Pay, timesheets and expenses

### 3.1 Timesheets

Fill in the timesheet app every day before you leave the last site. Lena locks the week on Monday at 10:00. A missing timesheet means your pay for that week is calculated on contracted hours only and corrected the following month.

### 3.2 Pay day

Pay day is the last working day of the month, by bank transfer.

### 3.3 Expenses

Fuel for company vans goes on the fuel card only. Small purchases on site (a padlock, a bag of fixings) up to £25 can be claimed with a receipt photographed into the expenses app. Anything over £25 needs a purchase order number from Lena first.

## 4. Vans, plant and equipment

### 4.1 Vans

Each crew has a named driver. Do a walk-round check every morning and log it. Report damage the same day, even a scratch — it costs far more to argue about later.

### 4.2 Hired plant

We hire diggers, chippers and access platforms rather than own them. Bookings go through Lena, who holds the hire account. Never accept a delivery of hired plant without checking it against the hire note and photographing it.

### 4.3 Small tools

Mowers, strimmers and hedge cutters are signed out from the tool store. Return them clean. Report faults with a red tag; do not put a tagged tool back on the rack.

## 5. Health and safety

### 5.1 PPE

Steel toe-capped boots and hi-vis are compulsory on every site. Eye and ear protection for any powered tool. Chainsaw trousers, gloves and helmet with visor for chainsaw work — no exceptions, no matter how quick the job looks.

### 5.2 Lone working

Nobody uses a chainsaw, climbs, or works near water alone. If a crew is split, the person on their own checks in with the office every two hours.

### 5.3 Chemicals

Only staff with a current PA1/PA6 certificate may mix or apply herbicide. Chemicals are stored in the locked bunker at the yard and signed out by the litre. Never spray within 2 metres of a watercourse or in wind above force 3.

### 5.4 Accidents and near misses

Every accident and every near miss goes in the accident book the same day. Near misses matter more than you think; they tell us what will go wrong next.

### 5.5 Working around the public

Most of our sites are open while we work: schools, care homes, hotels, retail parks. Cone off the area, keep blowers pointed away from doors and car parks, and stop if someone walks into the work zone. At schools, no work in play areas during break times, and no photographs of children, ever.

## 6. On site with customers

### 6.1 Signing in

Sign in at reception where there is one. Many sites ask for a contractor badge — wear it.

### 6.2 Talking to customers

Be friendly and brief. If a customer asks for extra work, write it down and pass it to the account manager. Do not agree prices on site and do not promise dates. "I'll get Marcus to call you" is always the right answer.

### 6.3 Complaints

If a customer complains, listen, apologise for the inconvenience, and tell the office the same day. Do not argue on site.

### 6.4 Keys and codes

Some customers give us keys or gate codes. Keys live in the key safe at the yard and are signed out. Gate codes are in the job sheet and nowhere else — not on the van dashboard, not in WhatsApp.

## 7. Waste

Green waste goes back to the yard for chipping or to the composting site on Mill Road. General waste from customer sites is the customer's responsibility unless the job sheet says otherwise. Never leave cuttings in a customer's bins without permission.

## 8. Holidays and absence

### 8.1 Holiday

Full-time staff get 25 days plus bank holidays. Book holiday in the app at least two weeks ahead. We limit holiday in April, May and the first half of November because those are our busiest weeks.

### 8.2 Sickness

Phone (do not text) Priya before 07:00 if you are unwell. Self-certify up to seven days; after that we need a fit note.

## 9. Training

Everyone gets two paid training days a year on top of mandatory certificates. Ask Priya. We pay for PA1/PA6, chainsaw (CS30/31), first aid at work and the LANTRA tree safety course.

## 10. Conduct

### 10.1 Phones

Personal phones away while operating machinery. Photos of completed work for the job sheet are encouraged.

### 10.2 Social media

Do not post photos of customer sites, customer staff or customer names without asking Dana first.

### 10.3 Drugs and alcohol

Zero tolerance. Anyone suspected of being unfit to work will be sent home and may be tested.

## 11. Leaving

Give the notice in your contract. Return your uniform, keys, fuel card and any tools on your last day. Lena will send your P45.
