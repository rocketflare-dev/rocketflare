# Site Schedule — Harbourside Dental Lab

Under the Brightside Master Services Agreement (2025 edition).

## Customer

Harbourside Dental Lab Ltd (a dental laboratory — makes crowns and dentures for practices; no patients on site).

## Site

Unit 9, Harbourside Works, Ashcombe AC1 4LB. Grass verge along the unit frontage and the shared planter by the door.

## Contact

Owen Pritchard, Lab Director — owen.pritchard@harbourside-lab.example — 01632 960230.

## Plan

Essential. Monthly Fee £120 ex VAT.

Winter gritting is not included; the estate management company grits the shared yard.

## Visits

Fortnightly on Thursdays (April–October), monthly in winter.

## Term and payment

Start date 4 November 2025. The contract renews on 3 November 2026. Invoices are payable within 30 days.

## Account manager

Marcus Bell.

## Notes for the crew

- The lab works with fine dust and chemicals indoors; keep the front door clear and don't blow cuttings towards it.
- The shared planter by the door is half theirs and half the neighbouring unit's. Only the half nearest Unit 9 is ours.
- Park on the estate road, not in the lab's two bays (they're for couriers collecting work).
- There is no outside tap. Bring water for the planter in summer.

Not to be confused with Harbour View Dental, the dental practice on Quay Street — a different customer.

## Site plan

The site plan attached to this schedule shows the areas we maintain outlined in green, areas to be gritted (if any) hatched in blue, and hazards marked with a red triangle. Anything outside the green line is not ours unless agreed in writing. Both parties keep a signed copy of the plan; if the plan and the description above disagree, call the account manager before doing the work.
