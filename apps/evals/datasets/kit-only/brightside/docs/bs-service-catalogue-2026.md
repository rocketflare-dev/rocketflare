# Brightside Grounds & Gardens — Service Catalogue 2026

Issued January 2026 by Marcus Bell, Account Manager. This catalogue replaces the 2025 leaflet. All prices exclude VAT. Prices are held for the calendar year unless a winter price list says otherwise.

## How our plans work

Every customer is on one of three maintenance plans. A plan is a fixed monthly fee, quoted per site, that covers a set of routine services on a regular visit day. Anything outside the plan is charged at the catalogue price below.

### Essential

For small sites with a lawn and not much else: front-of-office grass, a strip by the car park, a couple of tubs. Fortnightly visits from April to October, monthly from November to March.

### Standard

Our most popular plan. Weekly visits in the growing season, fortnightly in winter. Adds hedges, weeds and autumn leaves to the lawn work.

### Premium

For sites where appearance matters every day — hotels, retail parks, business parks. Weekly visits all year, seasonal bedding, and winter gritting on an annual plan so the car park is safe without anyone having to ring us.

## Service prices

When a service is not part of a customer's plan, or the customer wants more of it than the plan covers, we charge the prices in this section.

|---|---|

Spring or autumn renovation. Quoted per site after a walk-round; not part of any plan.

Pruning, deadheading and mulching of shrub beds is included alongside weed control on Standard and Premium visits.

|---|---|

#### Tree works

Crown lifts, reductions, deadwood removal and felling are quoted after a survey. Works to trees covered by a Tree Preservation Order or in a conservation area need council consent first; we apply on your behalf and the six-week consent period applies.

## Visit days

Each site has a fixed visit day so that your staff know when to expect us. We set it when the contract starts and keep it unless the round changes. If your visit day falls on a bank holiday we come the next working day.

## Weather

We do not mow in heavy rain or when the ground is waterlogged. A missed visit is rescheduled within the same week where possible. Plans are priced on an annual basis, so an occasional missed visit is not refunded; three or more missed visits in a month are credited at the per-visit price.

## Payment

Invoices are raised on the first working day of each month for the plan fee and monthly in arrears for extra work. Payment terms are set in each customer's contract; where the contract says nothing, the terms in our master services agreement apply.

## Changes to this catalogue

We review prices once a year. Winter prices are reviewed every September and published in a separate winter price list before the gritting season starts. Customers get at least 30 days' written notice of any change to their plan fee.
