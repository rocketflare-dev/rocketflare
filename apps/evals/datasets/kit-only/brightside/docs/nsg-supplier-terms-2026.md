# Northern Salt & Grit Co. — Winter Supply Terms 2026/27

Customer: Brightside Grounds & Gardens Ltd. Customer account: NSG-0093.

These terms replace the 2025/26 terms from 1 October 2026.

## 1. Contact

Ailsa Ferguson has left the company. Your new account contact is Duncan Reid (Sales) — duncan.reid@nsg-salt.example.

## 2. Products

Brown rock salt (6 mm, BS 3247), loose or in 25 kg bags.

## 3. Ordering

3.1 Minimum order: £500 ex VAT per delivery. This reflects the higher cost of haulage.

3.2 Lead time: 2 working days in season (November to March).

## 4. Payment

Payment within 30 days of invoice.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Loose salt is tipped where you direct, on hard standing. Please cover it.

5.2 Bagged salt comes on returnable pallets, collected on the next delivery.

## 6. Pre-season orders

Pre-season orders placed by 30 September are delivered in October and are allocated first after councils during a shortage. We strongly recommend a pre-season order this year: haulage capacity in the region is tight.

## 7. Price

Prices per tonne for 2026/27 are on the separate price schedule and are fixed until 31 March 2027.

## 8. Changes from last season

- New account contact (see section 1).
- Higher minimum order (section 3.1).
- Out-of-season deliveries are no longer offered from April to September except for pre-season orders.

## 9. Health and safety on delivery

Our drivers will not tip on slopes steeper than 1 in 10, under overhead lines, or within 5 metres of a watercourse. A banksman is now required for every reversing manoeuvre on customer sites; please make sure someone is present.
