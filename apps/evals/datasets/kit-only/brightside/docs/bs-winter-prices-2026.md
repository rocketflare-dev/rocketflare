# Winter Price List 2026/27

Valid 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027. All prices ex VAT. Replaces the 2025/26 list.

Salt costs went up again over the summer (our supplier raised its minimum order and its tonnage price), and fuel is up on last year. We have held the salt price for customers and put the increase on the gritting plan. Call-outs stay at last year's price.

## Gritting

| Service | Annual plan, per site | Single call-out |
|---|---|---|
| Car park and entrances gritting | £1,195 | £85 |

Annual plan: included in Premium; others can add it per site. Five monthly instalments, November to March.

## Salt

| Product | Per tonne |
|---|---|
| Brown rock salt, loose or bagged | £120 |

Half-tonne minimum. Bagging adds £15 per delivery.

## Notice

Customers already on the annual plan were told of the new price by letter on 1 September. The new price applies from 1 November 2026.
