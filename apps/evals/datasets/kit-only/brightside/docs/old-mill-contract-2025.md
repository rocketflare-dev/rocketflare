# Site Schedule — The Old Mill Hotel

Under the Brightside Master Services Agreement (2025 edition).

## 1. Customer and site

The Old Mill Hotel (Ashcombe) Ltd. Site: The Old Mill Hotel, Mill Road, Ashcombe AC3 1MH — front lawns, the riverside wedding lawn, the walled garden, guest car park and the drive.

## 2. Contact

Gregor Lindsay, General Manager — gregor.lindsay@oldmillhotel.example — 01632 960520.

## 3. Plan and fee

Premium plan. Monthly Fee £890 ex VAT.

Includes winter gritting of the drive, guest car park and the footbridge to the wedding lawn.

## 4. Visits

Weekly on Fridays, so the grounds look their best for weekend guests and weddings. Mowing to finish by 11:00 on wedding days.

## 5. Term

Start 1 July 2025. Renewal date 30 June 2026.

## 6. Payment

Invoices payable within 30 days.

Signed: Gregor Lindsay, 1 July 2025 / Dana Whitlock, 1 July 2025

## 7. Particular requirements

7.1 Wedding days: the events team will send a list of wedding dates each month. On those days the crew will not use blowers or mowers near the wedding lawn after 11:00 and will not park on the drive.

7.2 The riverbank is steep and slippery. Strimming the bank is a two-person job, with one person spotting.

7.3 The walled garden roses are pruned in February by our tree work lead, who also looks after the espalier pears on the south wall.

7.4 The hotel's guests must never be asked to move their cars. If a car is in the way, work around it and come back later.

## Site plan

The site plan attached to this schedule shows the areas we maintain outlined in green, areas to be gritted (if any) hatched in blue, and hazards marked with a red triangle. Anything outside the green line is not ours unless agreed in writing. Both parties keep a signed copy of the plan; if the plan and the description above disagree, call the account manager before doing the work.
