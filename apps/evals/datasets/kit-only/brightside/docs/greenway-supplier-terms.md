# Greenway Garden Wholesale

12 August 2025

Dear Brightside,

Welcome to Greenway Garden Wholesale. Your trade account is now open.

## Account details

- Account number: GW-7781
- Account manager: Paul Dunmore — paul.dunmore@greenway-wholesale.example

## Terms

- Lead time: 7 days from order (we deliver your area on Tuesdays).
- Minimum order: £250 ex VAT per delivery.
- Payment: 30 days from invoice.

## Range

Bedding plants, perennials, compost, bagged and bulk aggregates, bagged rock salt (winter only), hand tools.

We look forward to working with you.

Paul Dunmore
Greenway Garden Wholesale

## Ordering

Orders by phone, email or through the trade portal. The portal shows live stock for the depot; anything marked "grown to order" needs 21 days instead of the usual lead time.

## Delivery

One drop per order to one address. Additional drops to customer sites can be arranged for a small charge per drop. Please make sure someone is on site to sign for the goods; we cannot leave plants unattended.

## Returns

Damaged goods must be noted on the delivery note at the time of delivery. We are unable to accept claims for damage noticed later.

## Credit account

Your account has a credit limit, reviewed after the first six months. Accounts over their limit will be asked for payment before further deliveries.

## Opening hours

Trade counter: Monday to Friday 07:00–16:30, Saturday 07:30–12:00. Closed Sundays and bank holidays. The trade counter is at the back of the garden centre; please use the trade entrance on Greenway Lane, not the customer car park.
