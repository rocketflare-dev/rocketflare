# Grounds Maintenance Agreement — St Wilfrid's C of E Primary School

Site Schedule under the Brightside Master Services Agreement (2025 edition).

## 1. School

St Wilfrid's C of E Primary School, Church Lane, Ashcombe AC2 5SW.

## 2. Contact

Mrs Janet Cole, School Business Manager. Email j.cole@stwilfrids.example. Telephone 01632 960410 (school office, 08:00–16:00 term time).

## 3. Plan

3.1 Standard plan, at a Monthly Fee of £340 ex VAT, invoiced to the school in twelve equal instalments.

3.2 Winter gritting is included: the annual gritting plan has been added for the staff car park, the main gate path and the path to the Early Years entrance. Gritting will be completed before 07:30 on school days.

## 4. Visits

4.1 Routine visits are on Wednesdays.

4.2 During term time no machinery will be used on the field or near the playground between 10:30–10:50 and 12:00–13:15 (break and lunch). During school holidays the visit day may move to suit the crew, by agreement.

4.3 Contractors must sign in at the office and wear a visitor lanyard. All Brightside staff working at the school hold an enhanced DBS check.

## 4A. Safeguarding

4A.1 Brightside staff will not enter school buildings except the office and the caretaker's store.

4A.2 Staff will not speak to pupils beyond a greeting, will not take photographs anywhere on the site except of completed work with no children in view, and will report any concern to the school office immediately.

4A.3 The school will tell Brightside about any change to the site layout — for example a new outdoor classroom or a temporary classroom unit — before the next visit.

## 4B. Outdoor learning areas

4B.1 The wildlife area behind the hall is not mown between April and July. Paths through it are strimmed to one metre wide.

4B.2 The Year 2 vegetable beds belong to the children. Do not weed, water or touch them.

4B.3 The willow tunnel is pruned once a year in February by the school's own volunteers; Brightside removes the cuttings.

## 5. Term

Start 1 September 2025, aligned with the school year. Renewal date 31 August 2026.

## 6. Payment

Payment within 30 days of invoice, as required by the school's finance policy.
