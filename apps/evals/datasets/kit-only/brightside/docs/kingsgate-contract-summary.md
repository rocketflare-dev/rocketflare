# Kingsgate Retail Park (KRP) — contract summary

Internal summary of the signed Site Schedule, for the office and crews. Prepared by Marcus Bell, 25 November 2025.

## Customer

Kingsgate Estates Ltd, owner and manager of Kingsgate Retail Park.

## Site

Kingsgate Retail Park, Kingsgate Road, Ashcombe AC5 8KR. Customer car park (640 spaces), the service road, the landscaping islands, the frontage onto the A-road and the bus shelter.

## Contact

Simon Hale, Estates Manager, Kingsgate Estates — s.hale@kingsgate-estates.example — 01632 960701.

## Account manager

Marcus Bell.

## Scope in detail

### Car park

Sweep and blow the bays and walkways on every visit, empty the litter bins around the planting islands (not the shop bins — those belong to the tenants' waste contractor), and report any damaged kerbs or bollards to Simon by photo the same day.

### Landscaping islands

Twenty-two islands of low shrubs and ornamental grasses. Cut back the grasses in late February, mulch every island in March with bark from our usual supplier. Replace any dead shrubs at our cost in the first year, at the customer's cost after that.

### Frontage

The A-road frontage is a highways verge for the first metre — we cut it, but cones and signs are needed and only Gareth or Jamie may set them out. No work on the frontage between 16:00 and 18:30 (traffic).

### Bus shelter

Clear leaves and litter around the shelter; the shelter itself is cleaned by the council.

## Out of scope

- The tenants' own planters outside the shop fronts.
- Snow clearance with the tractor (quoted on the day, Simon's approval needed).
- Tree works — the six limes along the service road are on the council's TPO list.

## Notes

- Shoppers start arriving at 08:00 — no blowers in the car park after that.
- The landscaping islands have irrigation; check the heads before mowing.
- Big trolley park by the bus shelter — grit around it, not under it.
