# Ashcombe Plant Hire — Hire Account Confirmation

Account: APH-212 (Brightside Grounds & Gardens)

Contact: Lee Barton, Hire Desk — lee.barton@ashcombeplanthire.example

- Next-day delivery on anything in the yard catalogue: book by 15:00 for delivery the following day (1 day lead time).
- Payment: 14 days from invoice.
- Damage waiver charged at 8% of hire unless you hold your own hired-in plant insurance.
- Off-hire by phone before 10:00 to stop the clock that day.

## Hire periods

- Daily hire runs 08:00 to 17:00. Weekend hire is Friday afternoon to Monday 08:00 and is charged as one and a half days.
- Weekly hire is five working days.

## Operator-assisted hire

Chippers and stump grinders can be hired with an operator. Book at least two days ahead for operator hire.

## Customer's responsibilities

- Check the machine on delivery and sign the hire note. Photographs are recommended.
- Keep the machine secure overnight. Theft from an unlocked site is not covered by the damage waiver.
- Return the machine clean. A cleaning charge applies otherwise.
- Never refuel a machine with the wrong fuel. Diesel only unless the hire note says otherwise.

## Breakdowns

Ring the hire desk. We aim to repair or replace within four working hours inside the AC postcode area.

## Licences and training

Hirers confirm that anyone operating hired plant is trained and competent. Chainsaws, chippers and mini-diggers require the relevant certificate; we may ask to see it at collection or delivery.
