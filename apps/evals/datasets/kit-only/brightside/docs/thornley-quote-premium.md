# PROPOSAL (not a contract) — Thornley Leisure Centre

Prepared for Marco Bianchi, Centre Manager. Prepared by Sophie Tran, 7 May 2026. Valid for 30 days.

## What we're proposing

Following your visit to the Old Mill's wedding lawn, you asked what it would take to bring the centre's frontage up to the same standard for the summer open day.

We propose moving from your current plan to **Premium**, which would add:

- seasonal bedding in the six car park beds (May and October)
- weekly visits all year instead of fortnightly in winter
- winter gritting of the car park and entrance on the annual plan

## Proposed price

| | Current | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Standard | Premium |
| Monthly fee (ex VAT) | £520 | £780 |

The proposed fee would apply from the first of the month after you accept. If you do not accept, nothing changes.

## Why Premium for Thornley

The centre's frontage is the first thing visitors see from Station Road, and the car park beds are currently grass and shrubs only. Seasonal bedding would add colour from May to the first frosts and again with winter pansies and bulbs from October.

Weekly winter visits would keep leaves and litter off the paths by the entrance, which is where most of the slips reported to the centre happened last winter.

## What stays the same

- Your account manager, crew and visit time.
- The areas covered — the sports pitches would still be maintained by the council.
- Our master services agreement and your current payment terms.

## Assumptions

- Bedding uses our standard seasonal mix; a custom colour scheme for the open day can be quoted separately.
- Prices exclude VAT.
