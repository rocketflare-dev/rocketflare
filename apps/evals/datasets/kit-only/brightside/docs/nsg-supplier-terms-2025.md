# Northern Salt & Grit Co. — Winter Supply Terms 2025/26

Customer: Brightside Grounds & Gardens Ltd. Customer account: NSG-0093.

## 1. Contact

Your account contact is Ailsa Ferguson (Sales) — ailsa.ferguson@nsg-salt.example.

## 2. Products

Brown rock salt (6 mm, BS 3247), loose by the tonne or in 25 kg bags. White de-icing salt by special order.

## 3. Ordering

3.1 Minimum order: £400 ex VAT per delivery.

3.2 Lead time: 2 working days. During a named cold-weather alert, priority is given to councils and contract customers in the order their orders are received.

## 4. Payment

Payment within 30 days of invoice.

## 5. Price

Prices per tonne are on the separate price schedule and fixed until 31 March 2026.

## 6. Delivery

6.1 Loose salt is tipped where the customer directs, on hard standing, and should be covered by the customer. We are not responsible for salt lost to rain after delivery.

6.2 Bagged salt is delivered on pallets by tail-lift lorry. Pallets remain our property and are collected on the next delivery.

## 7. Quality

7.1 Brown rock salt complies with BS 3247. A certificate of conformity is available for each batch on request.

7.2 Salt naturally contains insoluble matter (marl). Lumps are normal and do not affect performance.

## 8. Shortages

In severe winters we may allocate stock between customers. Contract customers who have placed their pre-season order before 30 September are allocated first after councils.

## 9. Health and safety on delivery

Our drivers will not tip on slopes steeper than 1 in 10, under overhead lines, or within 5 metres of a watercourse. Please make sure the delivery point is clear and that someone is present to direct the driver.
