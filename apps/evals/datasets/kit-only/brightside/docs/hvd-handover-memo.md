## Handover checklist per account

For each account Marcus will pass on:

1. The signed site schedule and any letters that vary it.
2. Open quotes and promises made on site that are not yet in writing.
3. Known sore points — who complains about what, and what usually fixes it.
4. Billing quirks (direct debit, purchase order numbers, quarterly invoicing).
5. The crew lead's notes from the job sheet.

Sophie will check the CRM record against the signed paperwork for each one and flag anything that does not match to Lena.
