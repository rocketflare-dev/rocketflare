# Site Schedule — Ashcombe Business Park

Under the Brightside Master Services Agreement (2025 edition). Schedule reference BGG-SS-0419.

## 1. Customer

Ashcombe Business Park Management Company Ltd, on behalf of the tenants of Ashcombe Business Park.

## 2. Site

### 2.1 Address

Estate Office, Kestrel Way, Ashcombe Business Park, Ashcombe AC4 9BP.

### 2.2 Areas

All landscaped areas inside the estate boundary: the entrance roundabout, verges along Kestrel Way and Heron Drive, the central lawn and pond surround, the shrub beds outside Units 1–14, and the visitor car parks A, B and C. Tenants' private yards are excluded.

## 3. Contact

The Facilities Manager for the time being.
Email: facilities@ashcombebp.example
Phone: 01632 960300 (estate office)

## 4. Plan and fee

4.1 Plan: Premium.

4.2 Monthly Fee: £1,450 ex VAT, covering the whole estate as one site.

4.3 Winter gritting is included under the Premium plan for car parks A–C, Kestrel Way footpaths and the bus stop on Heron Drive.

4.4 Seasonal planting: the roundabout and the four beds at the estate entrance, changed in May and October.

## 4A. Pond

4A.1 The pond is home to newts and the estate has an ecologist's report. No chemicals within 5 metres of the water. Reeds are cut back only with the Customer's written agreement, and only between November and February.

4A.2 Fallen branches and litter in the water are removed from the bank with a grab; staff do not enter the water.

## 4B. Tenants

4B.1 Tenants' complaints about the grounds go to the Customer, not to the crew. If a tenant asks the crew for something, the crew should be polite and refer them to the estate office.

4B.2 The estate sends tenants a monthly bulletin; Brightside will give the Customer one week's notice of any noisy work (hedge cutting along Heron Drive, tree work) so it can go in the bulletin.

## 5. Visits

Weekly, on Mondays. The pond surround is done monthly, from a boat-free bank only.

## 6. Term

Start date 1 October 2025. Renewal date: 30 September 2026.

## 7. Payment

By agreement with the customer's managing agents, invoices are payable within 45 days of the invoice date, varying clause 5.3 of the Master Services Agreement.
