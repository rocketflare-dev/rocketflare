# Site Schedule — Thornley Leisure Centre

Under the Brightside Master Services Agreement (2025 edition).

## 1. Site

Thornley Leisure Centre, Station Road, Thornley AC6 3TL. Lawns around the building, the hedges along Station Road and the car park beds. The sports pitches are maintained by the council and are excluded.

## 2. Contact

Marco Bianchi, Centre Manager. marco.bianchi@thornleyleisure.example. 01632 960801.

## 3. Plan

3.1 Standard plan, £520 per month ex VAT.

3.2 Gritting is not included. The centre's own staff grit the entrance; the car park is gritted by the council.

## 4. Visits

Wednesdays, starting at 07:00 before the centre's car park fills.

## 5. Term and payment

Start 1 November 2025. Renewal date 31 October 2026. Payment within 30 days of invoice.

## 6. Account manager

Marcus Bell.

## 7. Particular requirements

7.1 The centre is open from 06:30. Mowing near the main entrance must be finished before the after-school swimming sessions start at 15:30.

7.2 The hedges along Station Road are cut in June and October. The Station Road side needs cones and a lookout.

7.3 The car park beds have a drip irrigation line under the bark. Weed by hand, no hoe.

7.4 The council's grounds team maintains the sports pitches. If our crew notices damage to the pitches (vandalism, moles), tell the centre office.

## Site plan

The site plan attached to this schedule shows the areas we maintain outlined in green, areas to be gritted (if any) hatched in blue, and hazards marked with a red triangle. Anything outside the green line is not ours unless agreed in writing. Both parties keep a signed copy of the plan; if the plan and the description above disagree, call the account manager before doing the work.
