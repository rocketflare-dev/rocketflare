# Site Schedule — Fenwick & Daughters Solicitors

Under the Brightside Master Services Agreement (2025 edition).

## 1. Customer

Fenwick & Daughters LLP, Solicitors.

## 2. Site

3 Market Place, Ashcombe AC1 1FD. Front lawn, two clipped bay trees in planters either side of the door, the rear courtyard.

## 3. Contact

Alice Fenwick, Partner — alice.fenwick@fenwickdaughters.example.

## 4. Plan and fee

4.1 Essential plan. Monthly Fee £95 ex VAT.

4.2 Winter gritting is not included. The firm has its own salt bin and will call us out if needed.

## 5. Visits

Fortnightly on Thursdays April–October; monthly in winter.

## 6. Payment

The firm pays all suppliers within 14 days. Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date, varying clause 5.3 of the Master Services Agreement.

## 7. Term

Start 1 February 2026. Renewal date 31 January 2027.

## 8. Account manager

Marcus Bell.

Signed: A. Fenwick, Partner, 20/01/2026 — M. Bell, 20/01/2026

## 9. Particular requirements

9.1 The bay trees are clipped to a ball shape twice a year (May and September). They are the firm's pride and joy; any damage is to be reported to Alice the same day.

9.2 The rear courtyard is used by staff at lunchtime in summer. Visits are to finish before 12:00.

9.3 The front lawn is small and is cut with a hand mower only. No ride-on, no blower on the steps (the firm's clients use them from 09:00).

9.4 Market Place has restricted parking: unload in the loading bay on the north side, maximum 20 minutes, and display the contractor permit the firm has supplied.
