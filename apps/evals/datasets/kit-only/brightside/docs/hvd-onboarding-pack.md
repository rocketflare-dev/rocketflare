# Onboarding pack: Harbour View Dental (HVD)

Prepared by Marcus Bell for Crew A and the office. First visit: Tuesday 17 February 2026.

Everyone in the office calls this one HVD or "the dental practice on Quay St". **It is not Harbourside Dental Lab.** Harbourside Dental Lab is a separate customer at Unit 9, Harbourside Works, Ashcombe AC1 4LB, on the other side of the harbour, on the Essential plan, visited on Thursdays. Same industry, different company, different invoices. We have already had one invoice go to the wrong one during the quote stage — please check the address on every job sheet.

### Access

The rear car park is reached from Chandler's Row, not from Quay Street — the Quay Street frontage has double yellow lines and no loading. Park the van in the two bays marked "Deliveries" at the back. If they are full, ring reception; do not block the dentist's own cars, which are the silver estate and the blue hatchback.

The side gate to the bin store has a combination padlock. The code is on the job sheet.

### Things to watch

#### Patients and noise

Surgery 2 has a window directly onto the front lawn. While the surgery is in use the crew must not run the mower or strimmer within five metres of it. Do the back first, then check with reception before doing the front. The practice opens at 08:30; ideally the front is finished before then.

#### The beech hedge

The beech hedge along Quay Street is about 1.8 m and cut twice a year (late June and September). It belongs to the practice up to the brick pier; beyond the pier it belongs to the flats next door and we do not touch it.

#### Raised beds

Two raised beds by the entrance, planted by the practice staff with lavender and rosemary. Weed them, do not prune the lavender unless asked. The practice is not on Premium so there is no seasonal bedding — if they ask, it's an extra and goes to Marcus.

#### Car park drainage

The gully in the north-east corner of the car park blocks with leaves every autumn. Clear it on every leaf visit and photograph it.

### Gritting details

The car park and side path are on the gritting round from 1 November. HVD is on Route 2 (harbour side) and is the last stop on it. Aim to have HVD done by 06:15 — staff start arriving at 07:00 and there is a ramp at the side door that ices badly.

Salt bin by the side gate: we top it up on the first visit each month in winter.

### Billing

Monthly fee is invoiced on the 1st. Gritting annual plan billed in five instalments, November to March. Extras go to Marcus for sign-off before invoicing.

### Checklist for the first visit

1. Walk the boundary with reception and confirm it matches the site plan.
2. Take before photos of every area.
3. Check the gully.
4. Note any hazards — the ramp, the step down to the bin store, the low wall by bed 2.
5. Leave a "Brightside was here" card at reception with the crew phone number.
6. Update the job sheet with anything the plan got wrong.

## Part 3 — Questions the practice asked at the walk-round

### Can you cut the lawn on a different day when we have an inspection?

Yes, with a week's notice. Ask Marcus to swap the visit for that week only. The normal day stays the same.

### Do you use chemicals near the surgery?

Only on the car park and path edges, and only products approved for use near public buildings. Nothing is sprayed on the lawn or in the raised beds. The crew will hand weed the beds.

### What happens if it snows?

The gritting plan covers frost and light snow: we grit before staff arrive. Heavy snow clearance with the tractor is not part of the plan; we will ring before doing it and charge it as an extra.

### Who do we call if the crew damage something?

Ring the office the same day. Every crew photographs the site before they start, so it is quick to sort out. We are insured for accidental damage.

### Can our staff put their own pots out by the entrance?

Of course. Tell the crew which ones are yours so they water around them rather than moving them.

### Will the same people come every week?

Usually. Crew A covers the harbour side of town. Holidays and sickness sometimes mean a different crew; they will have the same job sheet.
