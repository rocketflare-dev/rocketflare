# Greenleaf Horticultural Supplies — Trade Account

Account holder: Brightside Grounds & Gardens Ltd
Account number: GL-40217
Opened: 5 August 2025

## Your account contact

Kerry Walsh, Trade Accounts & Dispatch — kerry.walsh@greenleaf-hort.example — 01632 960900. Kerry handles orders, deliveries and any delivery problems.

## What we supply

Bedding plants, bulbs, shrubs, hedging whips, compost, bark mulch, grass seed, fertiliser, and certified amenity herbicides.

## Ordering and delivery

### Lead time

Orders placed by 14:00 are delivered within 3 working days to anywhere in the AC postcode area.

### Minimum order

The minimum order for free delivery is £150 ex VAT. Smaller orders can be collected from the depot.

### Plants

Plants are checked at dispatch. Report damaged or wilting stock within 24 hours of delivery with a photograph and we will credit or replace it.

## Payment

Payment terms: 30 days from the end of the month of invoice for approved trade accounts. Accounts more than 15 days overdue are placed on stop.

## Trade discount

10% off list on bedding and shrubs; 5% on bagged products.

## Returns

Unopened bagged products can be returned within 14 days for credit, less a 15% restocking charge. Plants cannot be returned once accepted, except under the damaged-stock rule above.

## Herbicides

Amenity herbicides are sold only to account holders who have sent us a copy of a current PA1/PA6 certificate for at least one member of staff. Keep your certificate on file with us up to date or herbicide lines will be removed from your orders.

## Seasonal ordering

Spring bedding must be ordered by 31 March for delivery in May; winter bedding and bulbs by 31 August for delivery in October. We grow to order and cannot guarantee stock after those dates.

## Depot

Unit 2, Hollins Road Nursery, Ashcombe. Collections 07:00–15:30 weekdays, 08:00–12:00 Saturday.
